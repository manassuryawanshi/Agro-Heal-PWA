# 🌿 Agro Heal — Hackathon Pitch Sheet

## One-Line Pitch
> **Agro Heal is an AI-powered bilingual farming assistant that helps Maharashtra farmers detect crop diseases, track live market prices, and get vet advice — all on their phone, in Marathi.**

---

## The Problem (Numbers That Matter)

- 🌾 **1.15 crore** farming families in Maharashtra
- 📉 **30–40% crop losses** annually due to undetected diseases
- 💸 Farmers lose **₹2,000–15,000/acre** selling at the wrong time without market data
- 🐄 **60% of livestock deaths** are preventable with early detection
- 📱 **72% of farmers** own a smartphone — but 0 apps serve them in Marathi with AI

---

## Our Solution

```
📷 Photo → AI → Disease Name + Treatment + Product in seconds
💬 Chat → AI Vet → Livestock diagnosis without visiting a doctor
📊 APMC Rates → Know today's price before going to market
🌤️ Weather → Farming-optimized 7-day forecast, not just temperature
📰 News → Relevant farming news in Marathi, filtered by their crop
```

## Tech Stack (Judges Love This)

```
Frontend  : React 19 + Vite 5 + Vanilla CSS
AI        : Google Gemini 2.0 Flash (multimodal — text + image)
Languages : English + Marathi (Noto Sans Devanagari)
Offline   : Full local fallback — works without internet
Deploy    : 1 command → any static host (Vercel/Netlify)
Size      : 334KB JS gzipped — loads fast on 4G
```
